Learn how to run powerful open-weight models (like Llama 3, Mistral, or Dolphin) entirely on your own hardware, build custom system prompts for specialized tasks, and create a fully private, offline assistant for coding, research, and automation. Built for developers, researchers, and power users who demand absolute control over their AI. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
